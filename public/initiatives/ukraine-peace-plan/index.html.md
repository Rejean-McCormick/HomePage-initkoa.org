# Ukraine Peace & Reconstruction Plan

**Freeze. Vote. Rebuild.**

This is not just a ceasefire proposal; it is a total reimagining of post-conflict recovery. The proposal replaces the deadlock of trench warfare with a verification-first settlement architecture and a reconstruction model built on transparency, neutrality, and operational sequencing.
