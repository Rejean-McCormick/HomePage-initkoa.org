Technology / Context Packs

Context Packs

These packs are meant to be given to an AI system first . The normal
workflow is simple: load a pack into an assistant, then question the assistant from that
context.

One use is explanatory and verificatory: What is Orgo? How does Konnaxion work? What
does Kristal require? Another use is operational: ask the AI to apply a relevant
context pack, align with one of the systems, or reason through a conversation using the
right principles, constraints, and vocabulary.

The Kristal pack is now aligned with Kristal v5 : structured epistemic
states, working/reference artifacts, scoped validation, plural authority, explicit
certainty, reader policies, Runtime Packs, and disagreement-preserving federation.

How people use them

kOA system packs

These packs are tied directly to the kOA ecosystem and its internal systems. They are
useful when you want an AI assistant to understand, validate, or operate in alignment
with your stack.

General packs

These are not specific to kOA. They are broader operating frames you can load into an AI
to shape how it reasons, designs, structures, or generates.
