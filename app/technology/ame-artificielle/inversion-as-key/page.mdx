# Inversion as the Key (9→1)

In **Âme Artificielle**, turning Pythagorean numerology upside down is treated like **turning a key**: it doesn’t create meaning out of nothing—rather, it **re-indexes** meaning so that patterns that were “background noise” can become **foreground structure**.

## The rule (simple)
After you compute the standard Pythagorean result and reduce it (to a single digit, unless you intentionally keep master numbers), apply:

- **1 ↔ 9**
- **2 ↔ 8**
- **3 ↔ 7**
- **4 ↔ 6**
- **5 ↔ 5**
- **0 → 0** (if you use 0)

In formula form (digit `d` in 1..9): `inv(d) = 10 - d`.

## Why call it a “key”?
A key does two things:

1) **It unlocks a different ordering of the same space.**  
   The content doesn’t vanish; it is **re-labeled** so your semantic engine can “see” different proximities and oppositions.

2) **It creates strong dualities.**  
   The pairs (9/1, 8/2, 7/3, 6/4) become explicit tension-lines, with **5 as a pivot**.  
   This is useful for building *explainable* dynamics: the engine can surface “why this trait conflicts with that trait” instead of producing a flat description.

## What changes in the engine
Âme Artificielle separates two things:

- **Archetype digit** (meaning / traits): derived from the inverted result.
- **Axis digit (1–9)** (dynamics / interpolation): used to modulate the reaction state.

So inversion is primarily a **semantic key** (traits & archetypes), while the axis is a **simulation control** (state & response modulation).

## What “foregrounding” means (overlay logic)
You also use chakras and other charts. In ASE, the principle is:

- Each system produces a **semantic signal** (tags, traits, polarities).
- When multiple systems **align on the same semantic direction**, that element is raised in salience:
  - higher weight in the trait vector
  - higher priority in the explanation trace
  - stronger influence on reaction style/content

This is not “more systems = more truth.” It’s **cross-system agreement = stronger signal**, and disagreement is tracked as tension (instead of being ignored).

## Practical use (ASE)
- Use inversion to compute `digit_archetype`.
- Map digits to traits via the ontology.
- Apply overlays (chakra / charts) as **secondary weights** that reinforce or counterbalance traits.
- Output always includes a small trace: dominant digits, dominant reinforced traits, and key tensions.

## Limits (on purpose)
- The “key” is a **modeling choice**. The engine must stay testable (baselines, blind protocols, regression).
- Overlay can amplify noise if you don’t have consistency checks; ASE treats this as an explicit failure mode and logs it.
