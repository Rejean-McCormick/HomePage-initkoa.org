## What “Kompendio” means in Konnaxion

Kompendio is the **reference layer**: the public repertory of patterns, charts, and integration maps that lets communities:

- run the same civic process repeatedly without reinventing it,
- keep rules legible and contestable,
- migrate/self-host/fork without losing institutional memory.

For ethiKos, Kompendio will be the **playbook + canonical formats** for deliberation.

---

## What ethiKos Kompendio will give users

When defined, ethiKos Kompendio should make it easy to:

1. **Run deliberation the same way every time**
   - consistent phases, roles, and outputs
   - predictable “what happens next”

2. **Produce decision-ready outputs**
   - a clear draft, options, rationale, tradeoffs, minority positions
   - a decision record that can be audited and revisited

3. **Protect legitimacy**
   - guardrails against spam, capture, and performative participation
   - transparent rules about how inputs become outcomes

4. **Reuse civic work**
   - “reference packs” that can be imported by other communities
   - versioning so improvements travel without rewriting everything

## Planned contents (draft outline)

### A) Process templates (repeatable governance)
- Deliberation lifecycle templates (phases, timeboxes, roles)
- Facilitation checklists and “anti-capture” practices
- Participation formats (consultation, co-drafting, consensus mapping, etc.)

### B) Canonical objects (shared formats)
- Proposal / problem statement format
- Evidence packet format (what counts, how to cite, how to contest)
- Drafting format (options, constraints, rationale, risks)
- Decision record format (what was decided, why, by whom, what changes)

### C) Quality and legitimacy checks
- “Minimum legitimacy” checklist (before publishing a decision)
- Bias / exclusion checks (who is missing, what was not heard)
- Appeal / revision pathways (how decisions can be challenged safely)

### D) Integrations (user-facing, not technical specs)
- How ethiKos outputs connect to:
  - **Kollective Intelligence** (decision readings)
  - **KonnectED** (competence signals, when relevant)
  - **keenKonnect** (handoff to projects and deliverables)
  - the **Commons** (publication/preservation of outcomes)
