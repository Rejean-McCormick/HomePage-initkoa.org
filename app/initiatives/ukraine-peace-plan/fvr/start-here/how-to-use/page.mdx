# How to use this book

This GitBook is organized for two complementary reading modes:

- **Narrative mode**: understand the proposal end-to-end (Freeze → Vote → Rebuild).
- **Implementation mode**: review mechanisms, legal pathways, and operational details as modular components.

## Structure conventions

- Chapters `02`–`04` are the **core mechanism** (Freeze / Vote / Rebuild).
- Chapters `05`–`06` are **cross-cutting enablers** (governance, verification, legal pathways).
- Chapters `07`–`09` are **application layers** (playbooks, risks, toolkit).
- Chapters `10`–`11` preserve **history and traceability** (essays, archive, decision log).

## How updates should be handled

- Use **Changelog & versioning** ([`00-start-here/04-changelog-versioning.md`](/initiatives/ukraine-peace-plan/fvr/start-here/changelog)) to track releases.
- Record major design choices in the **Decision log** ([`11-appendices/03-decision-log.md`](/initiatives/ukraine-peace-plan/fvr/appendices/decision-log)).
- Keep advocacy messaging and ready-to-use talking points in **Comms toolkit** ([`09-implementation-toolkit/04-comms-toolkit.md`](/initiatives/ukraine-peace-plan/fvr/toolkit/comms)) so the core stays audit-friendly.
