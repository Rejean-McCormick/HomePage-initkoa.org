# Education Module: Verified Competence

### The Obsolescence of the Diploma
The traditional academic model is an industrial relic. It sells "Prestige"—a static snapshot of potential—at the cost of massive student debt and years of theoretical confinement.

kOA proposes a radical shift: **Verified Competence**.
We replace the rigid 4-year degree with **"Kristals"**—modular, verifiable blocks of skill (e.g., *React.js*, *Conflict Resolution*, *Hydraulics*). Your transcript is no longer a piece of paper; it is a live, blockchain-secured portfolio of what you can actually *do*.
